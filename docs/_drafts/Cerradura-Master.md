Pah! The lock I put on the dinghy outboard corroded and froze.

![Cerradura corroido](
  {{ 'assets/images/2026/06Cerradura/IMG_1709.jpeg' | relative_url }}
)

It is very hard to cut it open. My giant bolt cutters that I carry to
cut free the rigging if it fails-- these barely make a dent.
The hack saw for cutting pipe and cable-- it is useless.

![Las cortas como si nada](
  {{ 'assets/images/2026/06Cerradura/IMG_1710.jpeg' | relative_url }}
)

The designers of this lock imagined someone trying to cut it.  They did not
imagine anyone bathing it in salt water and then leaving it to corrode for
weeks in salt air.

![El Dremmel de rescate](
  {{ 'assets/images/2026/06Cerradura/IMG_1711.jpeg' | relative_url }}
)

Fortunately, I have some big guns. I have the dremmel. The cutting wheel of the
dremmel freed my outboard motor in minutes.
